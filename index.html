FAQ

Got questions?

Is VXPlayer really free?

100% free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking, and no account required. Just download and play.

Why does Play Protect show a warning?

Since VXPlayer isn't on the Play Store yet, Android shows a standard caution for APK installs. The app is completely safe.

Does it work with every website?

Most video sites work great. However, some platforms use strict DRM or actively block embedded players, so results may vary.

How do I update VXPlayer?

Simply download the latest APK from the same GitHub releases link. Install it over the existing version — your settings are preserved.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. VXPlayer doesn't collect any data, has no analytics, and doesn't phone home. Everything stays on your device.
