VXPlayer

A fast, clean video and music player for Android. No ads, no tracking.

Download latest APK
Android 8.0 or newer

🎬 Video

Local files, direct links, HLS and DASH streams, with resume.

🎵 Music

Queue, playlists and background play.

⚡ Controls

Speed, A-B repeat, sleep timer, equalizer and night mode.

💬 Subtitles

Subtitle and audio track selection, picture-in-picture.

📺 Cast

Cast to your TV, plus screenshots and HW/SW decoding.

🛡️ Shield

Pop-up blocking in the built-in web player.

How to install

  1. Tap Download latest APK and open the Assets list.
  2. Tap the APK file to download it.
  3. Open it and allow Install unknown apps when asked.
  4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway.
