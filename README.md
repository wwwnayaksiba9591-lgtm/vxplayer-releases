# ▶ VXPlayer

**A fast, clean video and music player for Android.**
Built from scratch with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Media3.

## ⬇️ Download

1. Open the [**Releases**](../../releases/latest) page.
2. Under **Assets**, tap `VXPlayer-vX.X.apk`.
3. Open the downloaded file and install it.

> **First time installing an APK?**
> Android will ask you to allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager. Allow it once.
> If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **Install anyway**. This is normal for apps that are not on the Play Store.

## ✨ Features

### 🎬 Video player
- Plays local videos, direct links, HLS and DASH streams
- **Resume** from where you stopped, with a one-tap play icon in the library
- Side panel with quick actions: Sleep Timer, A-B Repeat, Equalizer, Speed, Night Mode, Mute, Loop and more
- Hardware / software decoding switch (**HW / SW**)
- Screenshot of the current frame, saved to your gallery
- Rotation control with rotation lock
- Subtitles, audio track selection and picture-in-picture
- Cast support

### 🎵 Music player
- Library of the audio files on your phone
- Queue, playlists and a Now Playing screen
- Background playback with notification controls

### 🌐 Stream and URL playback
- Paste any video link in **Open URL** and play it with the native player
- Supports direct files, **HLS (.m3u8)** and **DASH (.mpd)** streams
- Video web pages open in a built-in **web player** with fullscreen support
- Auto mode detects whether a link is a media file or a web page

### 🛡️ Shield protection
- Pop-up windows opened by web pages are blocked
- Web pages cannot open other apps without your permission
- Stream and web links are opened inside VXPlayer, not handed to other apps

---

## 🔒 Privacy

- **No ads** inside the app
- **No analytics or tracking** libraries
- **No account or sign-up** needed
- Your videos and music stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Internet is used only for links you open yourself, and for casting
